Returns & Size Swaps

7-Day Streetwear Exchange Guarantee

We want you to wear AURAMODE with total confidence. If your garment does not fit the way you desire, we provide a smooth size exchange process.

  • 7-Day Exchange Window: Requests must be submitted within 7 calendar days of courier delivery.
  • Original Condition: Garments must be unworn, unwashed, and free of fragrance, stains, or alterations, with all branded neck tags and packaging intact.
  • Limited Drop Policy: If a requested replacement size is sold out due to strict limited capsule drop allocations, store credit or an alternative drop selection will be issued.
  • How to Initiate: Tap our floating WhatsApp Concierge or email auramodeszn@gmail.com with your Order # and requested size.
